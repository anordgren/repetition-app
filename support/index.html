Common questions

How does iCloud sync work?

Repetition saves your workout data on your device and syncs it through your private iCloud account when iCloud is available. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account and have iCloud enabled on each device.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Open Profile → Apple Health. You can allow Repetition to save completed workouts and read your latest body weight. Permissions can also be reviewed in iOS Settings or the Health app.

How do I back up or export my data?

Open Profile → Export & Import. A Repetition backup contains your app data, while the CSV export lists your logged sets. Restoring a backup replaces current data on your synced devices.

How do I restore Repetition Pro?

Open Profile → Repetition Pro and choose Restore Purchases. Confirm that the device uses the Apple Account that originally purchased Repetition Pro.

How do I delete my workout data?

Open Profile → Export & Import → Delete All Data. This removes workouts, programs, custom exercises, and history from the device and iCloud. Settings are kept.

What should I include in a bug report?

Please include what you expected, what happened, the steps that reproduce the problem, and your iPhone model and iOS version. Screenshots are helpful, but remove anything you do not want to share.
