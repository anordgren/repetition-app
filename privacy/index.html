Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

The short version

Your workout content stays on your devices and, when iCloud is available, in your private iCloud database. Repetition uses limited, non-advertising analytics and purchase services, but does not send workout names, exercises, weights, repetitions, notes, profile information, or Apple Health data to analytics.

1. Who we are

Repetition is provided by Nordgren Studio ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Repetition iOS app.

2. Data you create in Repetition

Repetition lets you create a profile, routines, programs, custom exercises, workout history, notes, settings, and related fitness records. This content is stored on your device. When iCloud is available, it is also synced between your devices using Apple's CloudKit in your private iCloud database.

We do not use this workout content for advertising or send it to Firebase Analytics or RevenueCat. You can export a backup or CSV file; any file you export is handled by the location or app you choose through Apple's share and file interfaces.

3. Apple Health

If you choose to enable Apple Health features, Repetition may read your latest body weight and write completed strength-training workouts to the Health app. This happens only after you grant the relevant Health permission.

Health data is used on your device to provide the feature you requested. It is not sent to our analytics or purchase providers and is not used for advertising. You can change Health access at any time in iOS Settings or the Health app.

4. Analytics data

We use Firebase Analytics to understand broad feature usage and improve Repetition. Analytics may process an app instance identifier, device and app information, approximate interaction times, and coarse usage events such as whether a workout was started or completed, bucketed workout duration and counts, feature views, and purchase-flow events.

We deliberately do not include workout or routine names, exercise names, weights, repetitions, comments, notes, profile information, or Apple Health values in analytics events. Repetition does not include IDFA collection capability, and ad-personalization signals are disabled.

5. Purchases

Apple processes payments made through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to offer, validate, and restore Repetition Pro. RevenueCat uses an anonymous customer identifier and may process purchase status, product and entitlement information, app and device information, and the Firebase app instance identifier when analytics is configured. We do not provide RevenueCat with your name or email address through the app.

6. Notifications

If you enable rest-timer notifications, Repetition schedules them locally on your device. You can change notification access at any time in iOS Settings.

7. Support communications

If you email us for support, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, and attachments. We use it to respond to your request and retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for support, security, and legal purposes.

8. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell your personal information. Information is processed only as needed to operate the app through the services described in this policy:

  • Apple — App Store purchases, iCloud/CloudKit, Apple Health, and platform services.
  • Google Firebase — app analytics and usage measurement.
  • RevenueCat — purchase and entitlement management.

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and contractual safeguards.

9. Retention, deletion, and your choices

You can manage your information in these ways:

  • Delete workout data from Profile → Export & Import → Delete All Data. This removes workouts, programs, custom exercises, and history from the device and iCloud.
  • Delete the app to remove its locally stored data.
  • Manage Repetition's iCloud, Health, and notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence or to ask a privacy question.

Analytics and purchase records are retained by our service providers according to their settings, legal obligations, and privacy policies. Purchase records may need to be retained for transaction, fraud-prevention, tax, and legal purposes.

10. Children

Repetition is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, they use legal safeguards for international data transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Repetition or its data practices change. We will publish the revised policy here and update the effective date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Nordgren Studio at nordgrenstudio@gmail.com.
